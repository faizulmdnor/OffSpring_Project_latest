At home, I like to help my parents.
I believe it is good to be helpful and responsible.
Every day, I try to do small chores to make things easier for my family.

In the morning, I help my mother fold the clothes.
Sometimes I sweep the floor or water the plants.
In the evening, I help my father take out the trash.

During weekends, we clean the house together.
We wipe the windows, arrange the books, and mop the floor.
It is hard work, but it feels good to see the house clean.

Helping my parents makes me happy.
They always smile and say, “Thank you!”
I want to be a kind and caring child.
