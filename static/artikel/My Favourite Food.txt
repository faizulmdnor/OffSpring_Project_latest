My favourite food is pizza.
I like it because it is tasty, cheesy, and fun to eat.
My mum sometimes makes pizza at home.
She puts cheese, chicken, and vegetables on it.

I enjoy both thin crust and thick crust pizza.
Sometimes, I eat pizza with tomato sauce.
Other times, I eat it with a little bit of chilli sauce, but not too spicy.

When we have birthday parties, there is always pizza on the table.
It smells so good and makes me feel happy.
If I could eat one food every day, it would be pizza.
