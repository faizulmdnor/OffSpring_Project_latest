Cleanliness is very important in our daily life.
When we keep our body, clothes, and surroundings clean, we stay healthy and happy.
Clean places also look nice and make people feel comfortable.

At school, we must throw rubbish in the bin and keep our desks tidy.
At home, we should help clean our rooms and wash our hands before eating.
If we stay clean, we can avoid getting sick.

Our Prophet Muhammad (peace be upon him) taught us that cleanliness is part of faith.
That is why we should always brush our teeth, take a bath, and wear clean clothes.
We should also keep our toilets, kitchens, and shoes clean.

Let us all practise cleanliness every day.
A clean person is a healthy and good person.
