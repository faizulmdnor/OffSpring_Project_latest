My school is called Sekolah Rendah Islam Nurul Hidayah Penghulu Elang Kulim Kedah.
We also call it SRINPEK.
It is a nice school with friendly teachers and many happy students.
The building is clean, and there are trees around it.

I go to SRINPEK from Monday to Friday.
We learn many subjects such as English, Arabic, Mathematics, and Islamic Studies.
My favourite subject is English because I like reading and writing.

The teachers are kind and teach us with care.
We pray together, study together, and help one another.
There is also a big hall where we gather for events and talks.

Every morning, we line up, recite the du'a, and sing a song before going to class.
I feel proud to be a student at SRINPEK.
It is a good place to learn and become a better Muslim.
