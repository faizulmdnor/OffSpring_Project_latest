Yesterday, it rained all day.
The sky was dark, and the wind was strong.
I looked out the window and saw big raindrops falling.
The trees were moving, and the ground was wet.

“Can we still go to the park?” I asked.
“No,” said my father. “It’s too wet outside.”

So, I stayed indoors and played with my toys.
I also read a book and helped my mother in the kitchen.
We baked cookies together. They were warm, sweet, and delicious!

Even though I could not go outside, I still had a fun day.
Rainy days can be nice too!
