In Islam, girls are loved, respected, and protected.
They are a gift from Allah, just like boys.
Islam teaches us to treat girls with kindness and honour.

During the time of Prophet Muhammad (peace be upon him), some people did not respect girls.
But the Prophet taught that girls must be treated well.
He said, “Whoever takes care of two daughters properly will be with me in Paradise.”

Girls can learn, study, and become great leaders, teachers, and mothers.
They are smart, strong, and full of blessings.
Many great women in Islam, like Khadijah and Aisyah, helped spread knowledge and supported Islam.

We must not laugh at or look down on girls.
We must respect our sisters, mothers, and all women around us.
Girls are important in Islam, and they deserve love and care.
