One day, a big lion was sleeping in the forest.
A little mouse ran over his paw and woke him up.

The lion opened his eyes and roared, “Who dares to wake me?”
The mouse trembled and said, “Please don’t eat me, O King! I was only playing. If you let me go, I will help you one day.”

The lion laughed. “How can a tiny mouse help a strong lion like me?”
But he let the mouse go.

Many days later, the lion was walking through the forest.
He got caught in a hunter’s net!
He roared and pulled, but he could not escape.

The little mouse heard his roar.
She ran to him and said, “Don’t worry, I will help you!”
The mouse bit through the ropes with her sharp teeth.

Soon, the lion was free.
He looked at the mouse and said, “Thank you, little friend. You are small, but you are very brave.”
