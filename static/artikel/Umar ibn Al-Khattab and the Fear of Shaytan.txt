Umar ibn Al-Khattab was one of the closest friends of Prophet Muhammad S.A.W.
He was strong, brave, and always told the truth.
Before becoming a Muslim, he was against Islam, but when he accepted Islam, he became one of the best companions.

Umar loved Islam very much.
He always obeyed Allah and followed the Prophet S.A.W.
He hated lies, injustice, and anything that was wrong.

One day, the Prophet S.A.W said something amazing about Umar.
He said, “If Umar walks down a path, Shaytan will run away and choose a different path!”
This means that Shaytan was afraid of Umar because of his strong faith and honesty.

Umar never feared anyone except Allah.
He helped the poor, protected the weak, and ruled with justice when he became a caliph.

We can learn from Umar to always speak the truth, be brave, and do what is right — even when it’s hard.
