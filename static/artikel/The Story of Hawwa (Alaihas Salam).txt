Hawwa (alaihas salam) was the first woman created by Allah.
She was the wife of Prophet Adam (alaihis salam), the first man.
Allah made Hawwa from Adam while he was in Paradise so that he would not be alone.

Adam and Hawwa lived happily in Paradise.
They could eat anything they liked, except from one special tree.
Allah told them not to go near that tree.

But one day, Shaytan whispered to them and tricked them.
They ate from the tree, and then they felt very sorry.
They asked Allah to forgive them.

Allah forgave them, but He sent them down to Earth.
From Adam and Hawwa came all the people in the world.
They were our first parents.

Hawwa was a kind and strong woman.
She took care of her children and lived with faith.
We should love and respect our mothers, just like Hawwa was loved by Adam.
